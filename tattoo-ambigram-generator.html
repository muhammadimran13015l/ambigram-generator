What makes a tattoo ambigram generator

Tattoos demand thicker strokes, tighter kerning, and simpler silhouettes than a screen design — ink spreads and ages differently than pixels. This generator biases toward heavier weights and cleaner negative space so the result still reads clearly years from now — it also pairs well with a two name ambigram if the piece is for a couple.
