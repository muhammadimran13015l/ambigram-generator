What makes a mirror ambigram generator

A mirror ambigram reflects your word left-to-right across a vertical line, so the reflection reads as a word too — either the same word or a paired one. It's the most forgiving ambigram type to start with, since mirrored letterforms need less distortion than rotational ones.
