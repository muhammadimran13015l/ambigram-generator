About Ambigram Generator

A free symmetry design studio built for anyone who wants a professional-looking ambigram in seconds — no design software, no watermark, no account required.

Why we built this

Ambigrams used to require either serious typographic skill or a commissioned artist. We built Ambigram Generator to close that gap: thirteen focused tools, each tuned for a specific use case — a tattoo reference, a couple's names, a brand mark — instead of one generic tool trying to do everything at once.

Every generator on this site runs on the same underlying symmetry engine: a letter-compatibility map that decides which characters can convincingly double as another when flipped, mirrored, or rotated. We keep refining that engine, and the tools built on top of it, based on what people actually ask us for.

What you can expect

Free, always

Every tool generates unlimited designs and full-resolution downloads with no paywall or watermark.

No account needed

Type, generate, download. We don't ask for an email or sign-up to use any generator.

Built for real use cases

Each tool is tuned for its purpose — tattoo-ready strokes, logo-safe orientation, or dual-name pairing.

Get in touch

Questions, feedback, or a design that isn't rendering the way you expected? Reach us any time at ambigram.generator@gmail.com, or visit our Contact page for more ways to get in touch.
