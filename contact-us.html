Contact Us

Have a question, a bug to report, or an idea for a new generator? We'd like to hear it.

Email us directly

The fastest way to reach us is by email. We read every message and aim to reply within 2–3 business days.

ambigram.generator@gmail.com

To help us answer quickly, please include which tool you were using (e.g. "Mirror Ambigram Generator"), the word or text you typed in, and, if relevant, a screenshot of what you saw.

What to contact us about

Bug reports

A generator not rendering, a download that failed, or a display issue on your device.

Feature requests

A new ambigram type, style, or language you'd like to see supported.

Licensing questions

Using a generated design commercially, in a product, or in client work.

Press & partnerships

Writing about the site, or interested in collaborating.

Privacy requests

Questions about our Privacy Policy or your data.

Everything else

If it doesn't fit a category above, send it anyway — we'd still like to hear it.
