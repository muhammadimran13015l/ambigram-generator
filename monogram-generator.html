What makes a monogram generator

A monogram interlocks two or three initials into one compact emblem — not always symmetrical under rotation like a full ambigram, but built on the same letter-overlap logic. Popular for personal branding, stationery, and wedding motifs — or scale it up into a full logo ambigram.
