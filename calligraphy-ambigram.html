What makes a calligraphy ambigram

Calligraphy ambigrams apply symmetry rules to script and cursive letterforms — harder to pull off than block type, since connecting strokes must double as valid connections in both readings, but far more elegant for wedding pieces, invitations, and fine-line tattoos than a mirror ambigram in block type.
