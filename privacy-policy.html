Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This page explains what information Ambigram Generator collects, and what it does not.

Information we collect

Every ambigram generator on this site runs entirely in your browser. The text you type to generate a design is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to, or stored on, our servers.

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect names, passwords, or payment information, because no part of this site requires payment or a login.

Automatically collected data

Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance purposes. If we add analytics or advertising in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly before that change takes effect.

Cookies and third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a rendering library from a public content delivery network (cdnjs) to support the Download PNG feature. These third-party services may process standard technical request data (such as IP address) according to their own privacy policies. We do not use these services to identify individual visitors.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a data request can be sent to ambigram.generator@gmail.com.

This policy is provided as a general template and does not constitute legal advice. If you operate this site commercially or in a regulated jurisdiction (e.g. under GDPR or CCPA), have this policy reviewed by a qualified attorney.
